STAKING GUIDE

Start Earning in Four Steps

Everything you need to go from zero to staking on Movement L1 — from connecting your wallet to watching your rewards grow. Staking is how you put your MOVE to work. Instead of sitting idle, your tokens help secure the network while earning rewards for you in the background.

1

Connect Your Wallet

Head to the Movement Staking Portal and connect your Web3 wallet. Make sure you have MOVE tokens ready in your balance — these are what you'll be putting to work. Once connected, you'll see a list of delegation pools, each run by a different operator.

2

Choose a Pool & Deposit

Select a delegation pool and enter the amount of MOVE you want to stake. Your tokens will enter a brief pending state and activate at the next epoch — typically within hours. From that point on, they're live in consensus and earning staking rewards on every block.

3

Watch Your Rewards Grow

Here's the best part: Movement uses a share-based rewards model, so there's nothing to manually claim. As the pool earns rewards, the value of your stake increases automatically in the background. Your share count stays the same — your token value just keeps climbing.

4

Unstake & Withdraw

When you're ready to exit, simply request an unlock. Your tokens will remain in the pool — and keep earning rewards — throughout the lockup period. Once the lockup expires, withdraw your full balance, principal plus all accrued rewards, in a single transaction.

💡

PRO TIPS

COMPARE OPERATORS

Different pools have different commission rates. Lower isn't always better — established operators with strong track records are worth paying a bit more for.

DIVERSIFY YOUR STAKE

Consider splitting your MOVE across multiple pools. If one operator has issues, your other stakes keep earning uninterrupted.

CHECK EPOCH TIMING

Deposits activate at the next epoch boundary. If you're timing-sensitive, check when the current epoch ends before depositing.
